About this course

This course enables students to broaden their understanding of relationships and extend their problem-solving and algebraic skills through investigation, the effective use of technology, and abstract reasoning. Students will explore quadratic relations and their applications; solve and apply linear systems; verify properties of geometric figures using analytic geometry; and investigate the trigonometry of right and acute triangles. Students will reason mathematically and communicate their thinking in solving multi-step problems. Prerequisite: MPM1D.

  • A. Quadratic Relations
  • B. Analytic Geometry
  • C. Trigonometry

How your grade is decided

Evaluation focuses on the overall expectations of the course. Your work is assessed using a balance of the four categories of the Ministry of Education achievement chart. A variety of assessment techniques may be used, including in-class tests, quizzes, group work, homework assignments and in-class projects.

65%Classroom work
20%Final evaluations
15%Attendance and participation
  • Classroom work. Work you complete throughout the course.
  • Final evaluations. The mandatory final evaluations, including the final exam.
  • Attendance and participation. Coming to class and taking part.

Attendance

Come to every class. If you miss 3 classes in a row you will get a warning email. If you miss a 4th, you will be removed from the course. If you cannot come, tell your teacher.

Academic honesty

Do not copy from other students. Do not use AI or your phone during an assessment in a way your teacher has not approved. If you need your phone, ask your teacher before the assessment. Academic dishonesty puts your grade at risk and may mean you do not get the credit.
