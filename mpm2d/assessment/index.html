How your grade is decided

70%Term work
30%Final exam
  • Term work. Tests, quizzes and other classwork. How the 70% is split will be shared in class.
  • Final exam. One exam at the end of the course.

Achievement levels

  • Level 480 to 100%Above the provincial standard
  • Level 370 to 79%Meets the provincial standard
  • Level 260 to 69%Approaches the standard
  • Level 150 to 59%Well below the standard
  • Below 50%The credit is not earned

Attendance

Come to every class. If you miss 3 classes in a row you will get a warning email. If you miss a 4th, you will be removed from the course. If you cannot come, tell your teacher.

Academic honesty

Do not copy from other students. Do not use AI or your phone during a quiz, test or exam unless your teacher says you may. If you need your phone, ask before the assessment. Academic dishonesty puts your grade at risk and may mean you do not get the credit.
